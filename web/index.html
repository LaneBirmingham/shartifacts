PICTURES, SOUND & MOTION

Bring your artifacts to life.

Upload video, audio, images, captions, or data files (CSV, JSON, text, XLSX, PDF). Copy the embed code into your artifact source; artifacts read data files with window.fs.readFile. Files stay available across every version.

Up to 256 MiB per file. MP4 and WebM work best for video.

SHARE OUTSIDE YOUR NETWORK

Read-only links

Anyone with a link can view this artifact and read its data, but not change anything. Revoke a link to end it at once.

Sharing outside your network is off, so this server runs in private mode. To turn it on, set SHARTIFACTS_SHARE_ORIGIN to a hostname of yours and route it to port 8081; docs/sharing.md has the steps, or ask your agent.

WHO'S HERE

Who are you?

Artifacts greet you by name, keep your own data apart from everyone else's, and sign your comments. There are no passwords: anyone on your network can pick any name, so this keeps things tidy, not secret.
